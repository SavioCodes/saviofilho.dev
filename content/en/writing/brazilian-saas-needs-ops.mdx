## What I keep seeing

There is a tendency to treat smaller SaaS products as if they only need UI and a checkout page. In practice, even small products quickly hit the same system edges: auth, billing, quotas, uploads, webhooks, retries, and support workflows.

## What changes in the Brazilian context

The constraints become more local:

- Mercado Pago instead of Stripe
- WhatsApp in the core workflow
- mixed device quality
- teams that need pragmatic tools, not enterprise process overhead

## What I want my portfolio to show

I like building products in that space because the systems work is concrete. The best projects are the ones where business workflow and engineering concerns are tightly connected.
